## Start Here — Foundations

- 🇬🇧 **[Silence on the Wire](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/silence-on-the-wire)** by Michal Zalewski — Michal Zalewski's classic on the indirect attack surface: timing channels, protocol-stack fingerprinting, and the often-overlooked side data leaked by every layer of a stack. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1593270461?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[The Pragmatic Programmer](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/the-pragmatic-programmer-20a)** by David Thomas, Andrew Hunt — Thomas and Hunt's career-defining set of practical heuristics for writing software professionally — orthogonality, broken-windows, DRY, tracer bullets, and the underlying argument that craftsmanship is a posture, not a process. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0135957052?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Alice and Bob Learn Application Security](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/alice-and-bob-learn-application-security)** by Tanya Janca — Tanya Janca's hands-on AppSec primer covering threat modeling, secure design, secure coding, testing, deployment, and the social side of running an AppSec program — through a friendly, narrative-driven structure. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1119687357?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Click Here to Kill Everybody](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/click-here-to-kill-everybody)** by Bruce Schneier — Bruce Schneier's policy-level argument that as everything becomes a computer (cars, medical devices, infrastructure, voting), the security failures that used to merely cost us money will start costing lives — and the regulatory shape of that future is being decided now. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0393608883?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Crypto Dictionary](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/crypto-dictionary)** by Jean-Philippe Aumasson — Jean-Philippe Aumasson's alphabetical, opinionated reference on cryptographic terms, primitives, attacks and folklore. Snack-format companion to Serious Cryptography. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1718501404?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇫🇷 **[Cyberattaques](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/cyberattaques-les-dessous-d-une-menace-mondiale)** by Gérôme Billois, Nicolas Cougot — A clear, journalistic decoding of the cyberattack ecosystem — ransomware gangs, state actors, and the economics and geopolitics behind the headlines — by one of France's best-known cyber experts. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/2019462494?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇫🇷 **[Cybersécurité](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/cybersecurite-ghernaouti)** by Solange Ghernaouti — Solange Ghernaouti's broad academic survey of cybersecurity — risk analysis, governance, technical and legal dimensions — the standard French university reference, now in its 7th edition. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/2100841491?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Fancy Bear Goes Phishing](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/fancy-bear-goes-phishing)** by Scott J. Shapiro — Five famous hacks used as a way into the deeper question of why software is insecure at all, written by a Yale law professor who learned to code to write it. More a history and theory of vulnerability than a how-to. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0374601178?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Foundations of Information Security](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/foundations-of-information-security)** by Jason Andress — Jason Andress' compact tour of the field: confidentiality / integrity / availability, identification and authentication, network and OS controls, written for newcomers and adjacent disciplines. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1718500041?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[How Cybersecurity Really Works](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/how-cybersecurity-really-works)** by Sam Grubb — Sam Grubb's gentle, exercise-driven introduction for non-specialists who need a working mental model of attacker behaviour and basic defence. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1718501285?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇫🇷 **[La face cachée d'internet](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/la-face-cachee-d-internet)** by Rayna Stamboliyska — A lively, expert tour of the Internet's hidden layers — hackers, the dark web, Tor, Anonymous, WikiLeaks, Bitcoin — that demystifies the jargon without dumbing it down. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/2035936411?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇫🇷 **[La science du secret](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/la-science-du-secret)** by Jacques Stern — A lucid popular-science history of cryptography by Jacques Stern, one of France's most eminent cryptographers — from classical ciphers to public-key and the science of secrecy. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/2738105335?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇫🇷 **[Les virus informatiques](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/les-virus-informatiques)** by Éric Filiol — The reference French academic treatment of computer virology — the theory, algorithms and practice of viruses and malicious code — by Éric Filiol, a former military cryptanalyst and one of France's leading virologists. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/2287981993?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Linux Basics for Hackers](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/linux-basics-for-hackers-2e)** by OccupyTheWeb — OccupyTheWeb's introduction to Linux from the angle that hackers and pentesters actually need it: shells, networking, scripting, and Kali tooling. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1718503547?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Reversing](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/reversing)** by Eldad Eilam — The book that taught a generation how software actually looks once you strip away the source. Still the clearest on-ramp to thinking in assembly, even with dated tools. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0764574817?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇫🇷 **[Sécurité informatique](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/securite-informatique-cours-et-exercices-corriges)** by Gildas Avoine, Pascal Junod, Philippe Oechslin, Sylvain Pasini — A rigorous academic course on the foundations of security — cryptography, authentication, access control — with corrected exercises, from a team of well-known French and Swiss cryptographers. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/2311401688?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇫🇷 **[Sécurité informatique](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/securite-informatique-principes-et-methodes)** by Laurent Bloch, Christophe Wolfhugel — A principles-first treatment of information security for DSI, RSSI and sysadmins — architecture, cryptography, network defence and security policy — from two veteran French practitioners. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/2212137370?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇫🇷 **[Sécurité informatique - Ethical Hacking](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/securite-informatique-ethical-hacking)** by ACISSI — The French-language reference for offensive security: a thick, lab-heavy tour of the attacker's toolkit, maintained across editions by the ACISSI collective under the motto “learn the attack to better defend.” — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/2409009743?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Social Engineering](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/social-engineering-2e)** by Christopher Hadnagy — Christopher Hadnagy's broad procedural reference on social engineering as a discipline — recon, pretexting, elicitation, microexpressions, and the structured engagement model his consultancy operationalized. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/111943338X?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[The Art of Deception](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/the-art-of-deception)** by Kevin Mitnick, William L. Simon — Kevin Mitnick and William Simon's case-study collection of social-engineering attacks: PBX scams, helpdesk impersonation, dumpster-diving, the casual lies that sound true. The technology dates the book; the human side is timeless. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/076454280X?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Understanding Cryptography](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/understanding-cryptography)** by Christof Paar, Jan Pelzl — A genuinely teachable intro to modern cryptography that derives the math instead of hand-waving it, covering symmetric and public-key primitives without drowning you in proofs. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/3642041000?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇫🇷 **[Cybercriminalité](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/cybercriminalite-comprendre-prevenir-reagir)** by Solange Ghernaouti — Solange Ghernaouti's structured treatment of cybercrime — how it works, how to prevent it, how to respond — spanning technical, legal and organisational angles. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/2100858696?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇫🇷 **[Exercices et problèmes de cryptographie](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/exercices-et-problemes-de-cryptographie)** by Damien Vergnaud — A rigorous problem book for learning cryptography — over 150 corrected exercises with course summaries, for L3/master/engineering students — by a French academic cryptographer. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/2100852841?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇫🇷 **[La cybersécurité](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/la-cybersecurite-que-sais-je)** by Nicolas Arpagian — A pocket-sized primer on cybersecurity as a societal and geopolitical issue — threats, actors, stakes and policy — in the classic French “Que sais-je ?” format. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/2130652190?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)

## Cryptography

- 🇬🇧 **[Real-World Cryptography](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/real-world-cryptography)** by David Wong — David Wong's hands-on tour of the cryptographic primitives, protocols and pitfalls that show up in actual production systems, with deliberate attention to TLS, Noise, modern AEAD, and post-quantum. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1617296716?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Security Engineering](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/security-engineering-3e)** by Ross Anderson — Ross Anderson's comprehensive textbook on the design of secure systems, covering protocols, access control, side channels, economics of security, and policy. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1119642787?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Serious Cryptography](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/serious-cryptography-2e)** by Jean-Philippe Aumasson — Jean-Philippe Aumasson's working introduction to modern cryptography, written for engineers who need both intuition and enough mathematical depth to evaluate the choices a library is making for them. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1718503849?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[The Code Book](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/the-code-book)** by Simon Singh — A narrative history of cryptography from Caesar ciphers to public-key, told through the people and the wars that turned on broken codes. Still the best on-ramp to why crypto matters. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0385495323?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Cryptography Engineering](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/cryptography-engineering)** by Niels Ferguson, Bruce Schneier, Tadayoshi Kohno — A working engineer's introduction to cryptography that takes implementation pitfalls more seriously than most. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0470474246?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)

## Reverse Engineering & Binary Exploitation

- 🇬🇧 **[Hacking: The Art of Exploitation](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/hacking-art-of-exploitation)** by Jon Erickson — A from-first-principles tour of low-level exploitation that still teaches the mindset two decades later. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1593271441?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Practical Binary Analysis](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/practical-binary-analysis)** by Dennis Andriesse — Dennis Andriesse on the binary toolchain you can actually script: ELF internals, dynamic taint analysis, symbolic execution and instrumentation with concrete code-along examples. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1593279124?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Practical Malware Analysis](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/practical-malware-analysis)** by Michael Sikorski, Andrew Honig — Still the gold standard textbook for static and dynamic malware analysis on Windows. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1593272901?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[The Art of Software Security Assessment](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/art-of-software-security-assessment)** by Mark Dowd, John McDonald, Justin Schuh — The 1200-page reference on auditing C/C++ codebases for security: parsing complex memory and integer interactions, language pitfalls, and how vulnerabilities arise from interactions between layers. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0321444426?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[The Hardware Hacking Handbook](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/hardware-hacking-handbook)** by Jasper van Woudenberg, Colin O'Flynn — Jasper van Woudenberg and Colin O'Flynn (NewAE / ChipWhisperer) on real hardware attacks: bus sniffing, fault injection, side-channel power analysis, and the lab work that turns a black box into a known target. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1593278748?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Windows Security Internals](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/windows-security-internals)** by James Forshaw — Forshaw takes apart the Windows security model from the SRM and access tokens up through Kerberos, with live PowerShell you can run against your own machine. The most authoritative single source on how Windows actually decides who can do what. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1718501986?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[A Bug Hunter's Diary](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/bug-hunters-diary)** by Tobias Klein — Tobias Klein walks through seven real vulnerabilities he found and exploited, in the form of personal lab notes, what he tried, what failed, and what eventually shipped to vendors. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1593273851?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Evasive Malware](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/evasive-malware)** by Kyle Cucci — Kyle Cucci on the anti-analysis arms race: sandbox detection, anti-debug, anti-VM, packing, and the analyst-side tooling and tradecraft that get past those layers. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1718503261?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Gray Hat Hacking](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/gray-hat-hacking-6e)** by Allen Harper, Ryan Linn, Stephen Sims, Michael Baucom, Daniel Fernandez, Huascar Tejeda, Moses Frost — A multi-author breadth-first reference covering the modern offensive landscape: web, binary, hardware, IoT, mobile, cloud, and adversarial ML — the closest thing in print to a single-volume snapshot of where offensive security is. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1264268947?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Hacking the Xbox](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/hacking-the-xbox)** by Andrew "bunnie" Huang — Andrew "bunnie" Huang on the original Xbox: hardware modding as the entry path into reverse engineering, plus a frank account of the legal fight that followed. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1593270291?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Practical Reverse Engineering](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/practical-reverse-engineering)** by Bruce Dang, Alexandre Gazet, Elias Bachaalany — A working reverser's textbook from three Microsoft / Quarkslab veterans, covering the architectures and toolchain you'll actually meet on real targets, including the Windows kernel and modern obfuscation patterns. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1118787315?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Rootkits and Bootkits](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/rootkits-and-bootkits)** by Alex Matrosov, Eugene Rodionov, Sergey Bratus — Matrosov, Rodionov and Bratus on persistent, deeply-embedded malware: kernel rootkits, MBR/UEFI bootkits, and the forensic techniques that surface them. Strongly Windows-internals oriented. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1593277164?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[The Art of Mac Malware, Volume 1](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/art-of-mac-malware-v1)** by Patrick Wardle — Patrick Wardle's deep dive on macOS malware analysis: persistence patterns, injection techniques, anti-analysis tricks, and the macOS-specific tooling needed to triage real samples. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1718501943?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[The Car Hacker's Handbook](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/car-hackers-handbook)** by Craig Smith — Craig Smith's guide to automotive bus systems (CAN, LIN, FlexRay), ECUs, infotainment surfaces, and how to fuzz, trace and exploit modern vehicles. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1593277032?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[The Ghidra Book](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/the-ghidra-book)** by Chris Eagle, Kara Nance — The reference manual for the NSA's open-source disassembler, written by the author of The IDA Pro Book. Exhaustive on the tool, thinner on the craft of reversing itself. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1718501021?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[The IDA Pro Book](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/ida-pro-book-2e)** by Chris Eagle — Chris Eagle's deep manual on IDA Pro, the disassembler that defined a generation of reverse engineering. Useful even with Ghidra in the picture, since most malware-analysis literature still assumes IDA. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1593272898?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[The Shellcoder's Handbook](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/shellcoders-handbook-2e)** by Chris Anley, John Heasman, Felix Lindner, Gerardo Richarte — A foundational text on memory-corruption exploitation across Linux, Windows, Solaris and embedded targets. Pre-modern-mitigations in spirit but still the canonical introduction to the techniques the modern toolchain is built to defeat. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/047008023X?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[This Is How They Tell Me the World Ends](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/this-is-how-they-tell-me-the-world-ends)** by Nicole Perlroth — Nicole Perlroth's reporting on the global zero-day market: how exploits get bought, by whom, and how the gray-then-black market shapes which vulnerabilities get fixed and which get hoarded. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1635576059?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[The Database Hacker's Handbook](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/the-database-hackers-handbook)** by David Litchfield, Chris Anley, John Heasman, Bill Grindlay — Litchfield, Anley, Heasman, and Grindlay's exhaustive 2005 reference on attacking and defending Oracle, SQL Server, DB2, MySQL, PostgreSQL, Sybase, and Informix — the era when the database engine itself was the soft target. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0764578014?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[The Mac Hacker's Handbook](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/the-mac-hackers-handbook)** by Charlie Miller, Dino Dai Zovi — Charlie Miller and Dino Dai Zovi's 2009 deep dive into the Mac OS X exploit landscape — Mach-O, IPC, sandboxing as it then existed, and the early-Intel-Mac exploitation chains. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0470395362?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)

## Malware Analysis

- 🇬🇧 **[Countdown to Zero Day](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/countdown-to-zero-day)** by Kim Zetter — Kim Zetter's investigative reconstruction of Stuxnet, the joint US/Israeli operation that physically damaged Iranian uranium-enrichment centrifuges via a worm, and what its discovery revealed about state-level cyber capability. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0770436196?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[The Art of Memory Forensics](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/the-art-of-memory-forensics)** by Michael Hale Ligh, Andrew Case, Jamie Levy, AAron Walters — Ligh, Case, Levy, and Walters' canonical reference on memory analysis with Volatility — the technique, the tooling, and the operating-system internals it depends on, across Windows, Linux, and macOS. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1118825098?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Evading EDR](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/evading-edr)** by Matt Hand — A component-by-component teardown of how modern EDR sensors actually collect telemetry, and where each data source can be starved, blinded, or bypassed. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1718503342?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Malware Data Science](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/malware-data-science)** by Joshua Saxe, Hillary Sanders — Saxe and Sanders apply machine-learning techniques (classification, clustering, deep learning) to malware detection and attribution, with working Python code and real corpora. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1593278594?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[The Ransomware Hunting Team](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/the-ransomware-hunting-team)** by Renee Dudley, Daniel Golden — Investigative journalism on the volunteers who quietly cracked ransomware to free victims for free, while the FBI mostly watched. A people-first look at the early ransomware economy. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0374603308?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)

## Web & API Security

- 🇬🇧 **[The Tangled Web](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/the-tangled-web)** by Michal Zalewski — The deepest book ever written on the strange, accreted security model of the web browser. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1593273886?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Black Hat GraphQL](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/black-hat-graphql)** by Nick Aleks, Dolev Farhi — Aleks and Farhi on attacking GraphQL specifically: introspection abuse, batching, depth and complexity attacks, auth flaws, and the differences from REST that make GraphQL pentests their own discipline. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1718502842?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Bug Bounty Bootcamp](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/bug-bounty-bootcamp)** by Vickie Li — Vickie Li's pragmatic walk through the bug-bounty workflow, from picking a program and recon to reporting findings that actually pay out. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1718501544?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Hacking APIs](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/hacking-apis)** by Corey J. Ball — Corey Ball's structured approach to attacking REST and GraphQL APIs: enumeration, auth flaws, business logic, mass assignment, and the testing harness around them. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1718502443?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Real-World Bug Hunting](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/real-world-bug-hunting)** by Peter Yaworski — Peter Yaworski breaks down real disclosed reports across major bug bounty programs, organized by vulnerability class, so readers can pattern-match real findings rather than learn classes from textbook examples. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1593278616?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[The Web Application Hacker's Handbook](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/web-application-hackers-handbook)** by Dafydd Stuttard, Marcus Pinto — The exhaustive reference for web app pentesting, comprehensive but increasingly a historical document. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1118026470?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)
- 🇬🇧 **[Web Security for Developers](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/web-security-for-developers)** by Malcolm McDonald — Malcolm McDonald's developer-side primer on the OWASP-class issues, framed around real attacks and defended with code patterns rather than vendor products. — [_Buy on Amazon_](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1593279949?tag=b08dxcs91m-20)

## Social Engineering

- 🇬🇧 **[Ghost in the Wires](https://www.cybershelf.org/en/books/ghost-in-the-wires)** by Kevin Mitnick, William L.
